WHAT'S NEW:
-----------
You can now adjust prices by either:
  1. PERCENTAGE (existing feature)
     - Example: -15% decreases price by 15%
     - Example: +20% increases price by 20%
  
  2. FIXED AMOUNT (NEW!)
     - Example: -1 decreases price by $1
     - Example: +5 increases price by $5

HOW TO USE:
-----------
1. Start the scraper by double-clicking START.bat
2. Navigate to any store's export settings
3. In the "Price Adjustment" section:
   - Select "Adjustment Type": 
     * Choose "Percentage (%)" for percentage-based adjustment
     * Choose "Fixed Amount ($)" for dollar-based adjustment
   - Enter your adjustment value:
     * For percentage: -50 to 50 (negative = decrease, positive = increase)
     * For fixed amount: any dollar value (e.g., -1, -2.50, +5, +10.99)
4. Preview shows how the adjustment affects prices
5. Export your products with the selected adjustment

Percentage Mode:
  Original Price: $100

  New Price: $85.00

Fixed Amount Mode:

  Adjustment: -$1
  New Price: $99.00
